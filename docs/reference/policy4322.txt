Course policy:
    Attendence: You are expected to attend every class and not to be late. If you have
     four unexcused absences, each additional absence will lead to a reduction of your final
     grade. If you need to miss a class, please email Adam IN ADVANCE about this.
    Participation: You are strongly encouraged to participate in class, by asking for
     clarifications, answering questions, or in any other reasonable way. It is much better
     to provide wrong answers or ask weird questions than to sit quietly.
    Quizzes: will be at least one quiz in this course. However, not solving questions
     correctly on a quiz will not affect your grade. Instead, these are meant to evaluate
     how the class is doing.
    Homework: On most weeks there will be a homework assignment. These will be
     LeetCode questions.
    Exams: There will be no exams in this course.
    Project: There will be a project involving AI, coding, and algorithmic design. You
     will present your project in class.
    Grading: Attendance, participation, and not copying AI answers without a good
     understanding of those.
    Getting Help: Feel free to attend as many office hours as you like and for any reason
     — no appointment necessary for scheduled office hours. You are welcome to attend
     office hours even if you have no questions or you wish to discuss pineapples. You are
     also welcome to email Adam to schedule additional times to meet.
